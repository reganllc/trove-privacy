Trove Privacy Policy

Effective July 19, 2026 · Trove for iOS · RJE Consulting

The short version: Trove has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data. Your files and everything about them stay on your device. The one exception is optional online transcription, which sends a single recording to Apple only when you explicitly choose it (see Transcription below).

What Trove stores (on your device only)

Trove keeps the following in the app's private storage on your iPhone, solely to make its features work:

  • Your files: documents, photos, and audio you import, download, or receive.
  • Playback positions: where you stopped in each audio file, so playback can resume.
  • Playlists and play queue: the collections you create.
  • Browser favorites, history, and downloads list: sites you save or visit and files you download in the built-in browser.

None of this ever leaves your device through Trove. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, your device may include Trove's data in your own backups, under your control like any other app.

The built-in web browser

When you use Trove's browser, your device connects directly to the websites you choose to visit, exactly as any browser does. Trove adds nothing: no proxying, no logging on our side, no tracking.

  • Logins and passwords are handled by iOS Password AutoFill and the system keychain. Trove never sees, stores, or transmits your credentials.
  • Fraudulent website warning: the browser uses Apple's WebKit, which may check the addresses you visit against a safe-browsing service (operated by Apple and its providers) to warn you about known-malicious sites. This check is performed by the operating system, not by Trove.
  • Website data such as cookies is stored locally by WebKit so that sites work and logins persist; you can remove it by deleting the app.

Read Aloud (text-to-speech)

Speech is generated on your device by Apple's built-in speech synthesizer. Document text is not sent anywhere.

Transcription

Trove can turn a recording into text. By default this runs entirely on your device, and the audio is never sent anywhere. For low-quality audio such as voicemail, you may choose “Online” transcription for a specific recording, which sends that one recording to Apple’s speech-recognition service to transcribe it. Online transcription happens only when you explicitly choose it, per recording. The recording is processed by Apple under Apple’s terms; the developer never receives or stores it.

Sharing and AirDrop

Sharing happens only when you initiate it, through Apple's standard share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is entirely your choice; Trove does not record it.

What Trove does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No selling or sharing of data (there is nothing to sell)
  • No third-party tracking SDKs

Children

Trove is not directed at children. Because it includes an unrestricted web browser, it carries a 17+ age rating on the App Store.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Trove ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that version ships.
